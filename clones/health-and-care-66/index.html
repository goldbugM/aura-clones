Welcome to Sovereign Care

A family-run provider of residential, nursing and dementia care across Kent and East Sussex.

Find a home Get in touch

Welcome to Sovereign Care, a family-run business that has been caring for older people since March 2003. For more than twenty years we have treated each person as part of the family — with warmth, dignity and genuine, person-centred care.

What began as a single home has grown into a close-knit group of three care homes across Kent and East Sussex, yet our founding belief has never changed: that everyone deserves to feel safe, valued and truly at home in later life.

Our story

Since first opening our doors in March 2003, Sovereign Care has remained proudly family-run and independent. That independence lets us put people before process, invest in our teams, and shape care around each resident.

"Our vision has always been simple: to run the kind of homes we would be proud for our own family to live in — care that begins with the person, delivered by a team who treat every resident with the utmost warmth and respect."

— Radha Ravichandran, Director, Sovereign Care

Our values
