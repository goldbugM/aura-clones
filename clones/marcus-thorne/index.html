Creative process

From brief
to delivery.

One photographer on your project, from the first call to the final file. Four stages, one point of contact, and a schedule you can plan a campaign around.

01

Discovery

Where the campaign needs to land, and the references that get us there. A first call, then a written scope with dates and deliverables.

02

Direction

Treatment, casting, location and kit, agreed in writing before the shoot day.

03

Creation

The shoot itself. Marcus directs, shoots and films on the day, with a small crew and a light footprint in the room.

04

Handoff
