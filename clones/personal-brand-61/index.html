Problem

You’ve tried to fix your funnel before.

Most soloprenuers try one of three routes. Each one solves part of the problem and leaves the rest to you, and that gap is where your visitors stop trusting and leave without buying.

  • Building it with A.I.

    At its core, A.I. is just an upgraded version of D.I.Y. template that makes your high-ticket offer feel ordinary. When the page looks like everyone else’s, your price starts to look too high.

  • Rewriting the Copy

    When the words come before the brand work, your page starts reading like a sales script instead of sounding like you. And the harder it pushes, the faster the right people click away.

  • Hiring a Designer

    Without your brand and your buyer guiding the design, you pay for whatever looks premium this year, and your page disappears into a crowd of polished sites, forgotten the moment your visitor closes the tab.

Solution

This time, your funnel starts with YOU.

Your brand, your words, and your design come from one person, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

  1. 01

    Starting With Your Brand

    Before a single word is written or a pixel is designed, your brand gets mapped: who you are, who you serve, and why your offer is worth its price. Your funnel is built on that foundation, so it carries a presence no template or prompt can copy, and your price doesn't feel inflated.

  2. 02

    Writing in Your Voice

    Your copy is written from your own language and your buyer’s real questions, so the page sounds like the person your audience already trusts. It guides people toward a confident yes instead of pushing them, which is how the right people stay.

  3. 03

    Designing for Your Buyer

    Every layout, colour, and detail is chosen to make your offer clear to the person deciding whether to buy. The result is a page that’s unmistakably yours and easy to act on, and that visitors remember long after they’ve left.

About Founder

One person. Every part of your funnel.

I’m Subham Mundra, a web designer and sales copywriter based in India. I built my first client website thirteen years ago and have been building them ever since. Along the way, I taught myself copywriting, branding, and coding, because there was rarely anyone else to hand those parts of the project to.

I’ve run my business on my own from day one, so I know what it’s like to be the strategist, the marketer and the person doing the work, all before lunch. I also spent years working alongside soloprenuers, founders, and experts and I kept seeing the same thing: people who were brilliant at what they did, presenting themselves through pages that looked and sounded like everyone else’s. That’s the gap I close. When one person handles your brand, your words, and your design, nothing gets lost between handoffs, and you get a funnel that feels like you and works as hard as you do.

The problem wasn’t their work; it was that explaining it on a website feels like selling, and that’s the part they never signed up for. So I take that part on. I learn how they work, then build a website that explains each of your offers clearly, so the right people can find them and know where to start.
